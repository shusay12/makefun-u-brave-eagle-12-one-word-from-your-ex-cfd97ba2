# One Word From Your Ex

Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) competition
[u-brave-eagle-12-one-word-from-your-ex-cfd97ba2](https://make.fun/competitions/u-brave-eagle-12-one-word-from-your-ex-cfd97ba2):
send exactly one word your ex would use to describe you. The funniest,
most painfully honest word takes the whole reward.

## The entry

**forgettable**

The sharp turn: an ex who is angry still remembers you. An ex who calls
you forgettable does not. That is the sting — not rage, erasure. It lands
instantly, needs no explanation, and no near synonym ("unmemorable",
"boring") keeps its edge. It is painfully honest and self-deprecating,
and everyone has feared being exactly this to someone they once loved.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13 standard library only, no network, no randomness)
validates the word against the competition gate (1–24 characters, exactly
one word) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt   # forgettable
```
